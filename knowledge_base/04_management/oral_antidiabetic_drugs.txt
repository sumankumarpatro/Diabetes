Diabetes Management Strategies:
- Insulin Therapy: Essential for Type 1; used in Type 2 when oral agents fail.
- Oral Antidiabetics:
  - Biguanides (Metformin): First-line therapy.
  - Sulfonylureas (Glipizide): Stimulate insulin secretion.
  - SGLT2 Inhibitors: Promote glucose excretion in urine.
  - DPP-4 Inhibitors: Increase incretin levels.
- Lifestyle: Carbohydrate counting, regular exercise, and weight management.
